Admission Information

Applying to PCM is quick and simple — online or in person. Here's everything you need for the 2083 intake.

How it works

The admission process

1

Submit your application

Apply online in minutes, or download the prospectus and drop your form at the admissions office in Nadipur.

2

Prepare your documents

Gather your certificates, transcripts and photographs from the checklist below and keep the originals ready for verification.

3

Sit the entrance exam

Appear for the Pokhara University entrance examination on the scheduled date. Bring your admit card and a valid ID.

4

Selection & interview

Selection is merit-based on your entrance result and academic record, followed by a brief counselling interview.

5

Confirm your enrolment

Complete registration, submit your migration certificate and secure your seat. Welcome to the PCM family!
