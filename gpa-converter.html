How it works

Understanding SGPA & CGPA

Your SGPA is the grade point average for a single semester. Your CGPA is the cumulative average across all semesters completed so far. Both are calculated using the Pokhara University grading scale.

To compute your GPA: for each course, multiply the credit hours by the grade point of the obtained letter grade. Add these products across all courses, then divide by the total credit hours. The result is your weighted grade point average out of 4.0.

  • Grades range from A (4.0) to F (0.0)
  • A grade of D or below must be retaken
  • Contact the examination section for official results
